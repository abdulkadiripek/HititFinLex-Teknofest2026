HITITFINLEX V2.9 - CAMPAIGN URL RESOLUTION PATCH
================================================

Purpose
-------

V2.9 fixes one systematic archive classification conflict:

- A generic /kampanyalar/ URL is supporting evidence only.
- A specific title rule is evaluated before the generic campaign URL.
- High-confidence KART_KAMPANYASI predictions are no longer forced to
  DIGER_KAMPANYA only because the URL contains /kampanyalar/.
- Non-campaign model labels on campaign URLs remain in review unless a
  specific title rule independently supports the product.
- Genuine title/model conflicts remain in review.
- The live 771 documents, frontend, NER rules and database schema are kept.
