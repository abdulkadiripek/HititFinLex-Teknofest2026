HITITFINLEX V3.0 - HISTORICAL NER QUALITY PATCH
===============================================

V3.0 preserves the frontend, the 771 live documents and the V2.9 classifier.
It fixes historical fact extraction quality before any database write.

Main fixes
----------

- Separates spend thresholds and rewards using local amount context.
- Rejects incomplete date-range fragments.
- Extracts full Turkish date ranges with or without a year.
- Accepts explicit USD/EUR finance limits.
- Rejects insurance policy limits as finance amounts.
- Recognizes compact maturity ranges such as 5-48 Ay.
- Recognizes percentage cashback as a discount rate.
- Deduplicates normalized NER and deterministic-rule facts.
- Skips incomplete campaign eligibility fragments.
