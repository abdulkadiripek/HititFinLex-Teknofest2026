# Third-party model attribution

The versioned HititFinLex NER and classifier weights are task-specific
fine-tunes of `dbmdz/bert-base-turkish-cased` (BERTurk).

- Upstream model: <https://huggingface.co/dbmdz/bert-base-turkish-cased>
- Upstream source and license: <https://github.com/dbmdz/berts>
- Upstream license: MIT

The original training metadata did not retain an immutable upstream model
revision. The links above identify the upstream project, while the exact
HititFinLex release bytes are fixed by the SHA-256 release manifest.
